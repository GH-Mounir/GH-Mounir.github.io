### Environmental & Radio Frequency Context Analytics

> **Core Principle: Experience ≠ Video Quality Alone**

#### Context Observability Layers
- **Geospatial & Mobility**: Velocity vectors, trajectory shifts, and transit handovers.
- **Atmospheric & Weather**: Ambient temperature, precipitation, and environmental comfort.
- **RF & Cellular Telemetry**: LTE/5G RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, and cell towers.
- **Device Hardware Context**: Viewport dimensions, battery state, thermal dynamics, and brightness.
