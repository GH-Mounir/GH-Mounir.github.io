### Latent Cognitive & Behavioral State Inference

**Core Goal**: Infer hidden user states from observable interaction events.

#### Modeled Latent Dimensions
- **Curiosity**: Exploratory search patterns and novel content seeking.
- **Hesitation**: Deliberation latencies and interaction pauses.
- **Choice Volatility**: Sequential preference shifts and playlist churn.
- **Decision Conflict**: Rating ambiguity and conflicting subjective evidence.
- **Engagement Trajectories**: Attention drift and immersion evolution.
