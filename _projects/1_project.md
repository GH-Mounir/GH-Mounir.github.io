### Multimodal Event-Centric Dataset for Human Experience Modeling

**IoB-QoE Suite** is a multimodal in-the-wild dataset capturing:
- **44 participants**
- **1,112 viewing sessions**
- **40,000+ behavioral and system events**

Collected across:
- **Human factors** (Subjective evaluations, perceptual scores)
- **System telemetry** (Buffer dynamics, bitrate switches, rendering jitter)
- **Network observations** (Throughput, packet loss, latency)
- **Context variables** (Geospatial, RF conditions, device orientation)
- **Content characteristics** (Spatial and temporal complexity)

---

### Core Research Questions
1. *How do human judgments and subjective QoE emerge over continuous observation?*
2. *Can latent user states be inferred reliably from granular event traces?*
3. *How can observable micro-behaviors explain subjective experience and dissatisfaction?*
