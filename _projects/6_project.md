### Multimedia Content & Video Dynamics Profiling

A specialized content-side profiling engine evaluating frame-level spatial-temporal complexity and bitrate volatility across diverse video categories.

#### Core Analysis
- **Spatial Information (SI) & Temporal Information (TI)** complexity curves.
- **Bitrate Dynamics & GOP Structure**: Micro-burst analysis under variable encoding presets.
- **UGC vs. Professional Video**: Characterization of compression artifacts across User-Generated Content.
- **Codec Benchmarking**: Standardized evaluation across MPEG-DASH, AVC, HEVC, and AV1.
