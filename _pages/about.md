Independent Researcher
Tunis, Tunisia

I am an independent researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, Bayesian cognition, multimedia quality of experience, and ordinal decision modeling. My research focuses on interpretable machine learning, uncertainty-aware inference, and intelligent systems for next-generation communication and multimedia environments.

My work explores how probabilistic reasoning, ordinal feedback, and ultrametric belief structures can support more transparent, robust, and cognitively grounded AI in decentralized and real-time settings. I am currently seeking a PhD opportunity to advance this research agenda and contribute to the development of human-centered intelligent systems.
